Future of work

The productivity illusion

We were promised AI would save us time. So why are we all working more?

If a presentation used to take two days, today people expect three in a day. The technology really does save time, but the market's standard just jumped up.

The most critical skill now isn't doing things faster. It's knowing how to set boundaries and choose what not to spend your time on.
