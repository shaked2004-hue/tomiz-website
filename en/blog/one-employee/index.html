When everything depends on one employee

Do you have one employee everyone turns to?

It looks like an asset. In practice, it's a risk. When all the knowledge and experience is buried with one person, everyone queues up at their desk and the business depends on them.

The goal isn't to clone the person. The goal is to turn their knowledge into a system: simple decision aids in the field that let more people reach the same level of performance.
