Privacy policy

tomizs.com is operated by Shaked Volozinski Lanes (TomiZ). This policy explains what information the site collects and what is done with it.

What we collect

Information you enter yourself in the forms: your name, phone number and email address, and in the contact form also your organization and message, if you choose to fill them in.

Cookies and the Meta Pixel

This site uses the Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram). The pixel stores cookies in your browser and sends Meta technical information about your visit, such as the pages you viewed, so we can measure our advertising and show TomiZ ads to people who visited the site. It does not include the details you enter in the forms. You can block cookies in your browser settings and manage your ad preferences in your Meta account settings.

Why we use it

  • To get back to you after you reach out.
  • To send you a guide you requested.
  • To send tips and updates, only if you ticked that you'd like them. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to the email or writing to office@tomizs.com.

Where it is stored

Your details pass through the automation service Make and are stored in TomiZ's work systems. The site is hosted on Netlify. Your information is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information stored about you by writing to office@tomizs.com.
