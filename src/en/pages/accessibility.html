Accessibility statement

It matters to me that everyone who visits this site can use it comfortably. The site was built to follow the WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility guidelines as closely as possible:

  • A clear heading structure and full keyboard navigation, with a visible focus indicator.
  • Sufficient color contrast and resizable text.
  • Alternative text for images and labels for every form field.
  • Respect for the operating system's "reduce motion" setting: animations are turned off.
  • An accessibility menu in the side bar: larger text, high contrast, link highlighting, a readable font and stopping animations.
  • A "Skip to content" link at the start of every page.
