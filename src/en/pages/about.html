Nice to meet you

I'm a learning experience designer, workshop facilitator and speaker. At TomiZ I help organizations and experts take complex knowledge and turn it into something people actually learn, remember and apply.

I develop and facilitate workshops, seminars and talks for organizations, teams and communities, specializing in bringing generative AI (GenAI) into work and learning, and in making complex knowledge clear, practical and engaging.

My work always starts with the same question: what do people need to be able to do after the learning? From there I build backwards, from needs analysis, through experience design, to checking that the training actually works in the field.

What guides me

Learning is measured in performance

The goal isn't for people to "complete" a training. It's for them to do something differently tomorrow morning.

Knowledge shouldn't be stuck with one person

When all the knowledge sits with one expert, everyone queues up at their desk. We turn that knowledge into a system more people can use.

AI with a method

AI tools save time only when there's a methodology behind them. Without one, they just create more noise.
